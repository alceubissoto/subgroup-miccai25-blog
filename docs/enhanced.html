🔍 Key Findings

Our evaluation on CheXpertPlus and SLICE3D datasets revealed that subgroups discovered directly from data showed significantly larger performance gaps compared to traditional metadata-based groups. The discovered subgroups often aligned with meaningful visual features rather than patient demographics.
